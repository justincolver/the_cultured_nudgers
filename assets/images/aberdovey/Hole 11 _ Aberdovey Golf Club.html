Pro’s Tip

A strategic tee shot is imperative, avoiding the right side at all costs. For those confident in their abilities, clearing the centre fairway bunker is the optimal play. Approach shots must be precise, accounting for the challenging green contours. Securing par here is a commendable achievement.
